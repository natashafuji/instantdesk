FAQ

Straight answers.
No technical fog.

Tell us what your current lead process looks like. You do not need to know what should be automated.

What is InstantDesk?+

Practical AI-powered automations that reduce repetitive admin and help businesses manage customer enquiries more efficiently.

Will it replace our current process?+

Not necessarily. We work with the tools and processes you already use wherever practical.

Do we need technical knowledge?+

No. InstantDesk is a done-for-you service covering setup, integration, hosting and maintenance.

Can it be customised?+

Yes. Your setup is configured around your enquiry sources, customer journey and desired actions.

What if a lead needs a person?+

The workflow can hand a lead to your team whenever attention, judgement or a personal conversation is required.

Will customers know they are interacting with automation?+

The goal is not to make your customer experience feel robotic. Automated communication can reflect your business’s tone and fit naturally into the existing customer journey.

Does InstantDesk use AI?+

Yes, where it adds genuine value, alongside reliable rule-based automation. The objective is a faster, more consistent process, not adding AI simply for the label.

Can we still see and manage our leads?+

Yes. Lead information and status can remain visible in the tools used for your workflow, allowing your team to monitor progress and step in when needed.

What happens after setup?+

InstantDesk can host and maintain the automation after launch. The ongoing support arrangement depends on the workflow and systems involved.

How is business and customer information handled?+

Data handling depends on the workflow and connected services. We aim to minimise unnecessary collection and use reputable third-party platforms with appropriate access controls.

How long does setup take?+

Timing depends on the existing lead process, required integrations and level of customisation. We confirm a clearer timeframe after reviewing your workflow.

What kinds of businesses is InstantDesk for?+

It is particularly useful for service businesses where enquiries are valuable, quick responses matter and teams spend time manually capturing or following up with leads.

What if we are not sure what can be automated?+

You do not need to know before contacting us. Tell us where the process feels repetitive, slow or difficult to manage and we will identify where automation may be useful.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing depends on workflow complexity, integrations and customisation. We recommend a solution after understanding your process.

How do we get started?+

Tell us how you manage enquiries. We will identify useful automation opportunities and discuss the next step.
